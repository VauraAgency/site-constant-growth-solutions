Partner FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers for business owners considering a CGS partnership.

What does Constant Growth Solutions do?

CGS helps businesses give their customers a clear, trusted path to the next resource they need. We identify where customer needs extend beyond your core service, build a partner-specific referral pathway, coordinate the handoff, and support communication through the process.

Is CGS an insurance agency?

No. CGS coordinates the partnership and the customer handoff. Insurance services are provided by appropriately licensed professionals working through Clearview Insurance. Real estate services are provided by appropriately licensed real estate professionals.

Does my business need an insurance or real estate license to partner with CGS?

No. Your team keeps providing its core service and stays visible as the originating relationship. The licensed professionals handle anything regulated or specialized, so you do not have to build another department.

What does the partnership cost?

Terms depend on the partnership model that fits your business. We discuss this during your introductory consultation, so you know exactly what to expect before anything starts.

How does a customer referral actually work?

Your team identifies a need and the customer chooses to connect through the agreed intake or referral step. CGS routes the request to the right licensed professional, coordinates the handoff, and keeps communication clear until the customer is taken care of.

Will my business stay involved with my customers?

Yes. You stay visible as the relationship the customer already trusts, without having to manage the workflow. CGS handles coordination and follow-through.

What types of businesses do you partner with?

Auto dealerships, mortgage professionals, real estate organizations, property management companies, financial institutions, builders and property professionals, and employers or other customer-focused businesses.

What do my customers experience?

A simple, professional introduction to a qualified professional, clear communication, and no pressure. Customers are never required to use the pathway and can choose whether to connect.

How is customer information handled?

Customers choose to connect through the agreed step, and their information is used to help with their request. We can walk through the specifics of intake and privacy with you during your consultation.

Where does CGS operate?

Services are currently offered in Minnesota. Our office is in Minneapolis, and we work with partners across the state.

How do I get started?

Tell us about your business and where your customers need extra support using our contact form, or request a quote on behalf of a customer. A member of the CGS partnership team will reach out to schedule an introductory consultation.

Still have questions?

We are happy to talk through your situation.
