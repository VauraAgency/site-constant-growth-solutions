Insurance for Individuals

Owner-Occupied Dwelling Insurance

Dwelling coverage for homes you live in yourself.

Some homes need a different approach than a standard homeowners policy. Owner-occupied dwelling coverage is built for homeowners who live in the property.

What it can cover

  • Coverage for the structure of your home
  • Coverage for other structures on the property
  • Options for personal property and liability
  • Flexible choices for older or unique homes

Who it is for

  • Owners of older or hard-to-place homes
  • Homeowners who need an alternative to a standard policy
  • Buyers purchasing a home that is not eligible for standard coverage

Coverage and eligibility vary by carrier and property, so a review of the home is important.
