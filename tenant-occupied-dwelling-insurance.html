Tenant-Occupied Dwelling Insurance

Coverage for rental homes occupied by tenants.

If you rent out a property, your needs are different from those of an owner-occupant. Tenant-occupied dwelling coverage is designed for landlords.

What it can cover

  • Coverage for the rental building
  • Liability protection for landlords
  • Loss of rental income options where available
  • Coverage for other structures on the property
  • Options for single-family and small multi-unit rentals

Who it is for

  • Landlords with one or a few rental properties
  • Real estate investors
  • Owners who recently converted a home to a rental

Landlords may also want to ask about umbrella coverage and encourage tenants to carry renters insurance.
