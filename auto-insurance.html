Insurance for Individuals

Auto Insurance

Coverage for the vehicles and drivers in your household.

Minnesota drivers deal with long winters, deer, and busy highways. Auto insurance helps protect you and your finances after an accident, theft, or weather damage.

What it can cover

  • Liability coverage for injuries and property damage you cause to others
  • Collision coverage for damage to your vehicle in an accident
  • Comprehensive coverage for theft, hail, fire, and animal strikes
  • Uninsured and underinsured motorist protection
  • Medical payments and no-fault benefits as required in Minnesota
  • Optional rental reimbursement and roadside assistance

Who it is for

  • Drivers buying or leasing a new or used vehicle
  • Households with multiple drivers or vehicles
  • Anyone who wants to review a renewal price or coverage limits

Rates depend on driving history, vehicle, location, and the coverage limits you choose. Bundling with home or renters coverage can often reduce the total cost.
